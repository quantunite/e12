Help

Questions?Start here.

What exactly is E-12?

A private members network. Each gate is a different club with its own benefits. Travel is open now, through LyfestyleX. More gates open over time, and your membership covers all of them.

How do I join?

Press any gold button that says Join or Become a member. It opens the enrolment form in a new tab. Fill it in and you are a member.

Do I have to join to book travel?

No. Go into the travel gate and use the customer link there if you only want to book a trip. Members get better pricing and the ability to earn.

Can I do all of this in Spanish?

Yes. Press "Español" at the top of the page and the whole site changes. Press "English" to change it back.
