Help

Questions?Start here.

What exactly is E-12?

A private members network. Each club is separate, with its own membership and its own benefits. Travel is open now, through LyfestyleX. More clubs open over time, and you join the ones you want.

How do I join?

Open the club you want, then press its gold Join button. That opens the enrolment form in a new tab. Fill it in and you are a member of that club.

Do I have to join to book travel?

No. Go into the travel club and use the customer link there if you only want to book a trip. Members get better pricing and the ability to earn.

Can I do all of this in Spanish?

Yes. Press "Español" at the top of the page and the whole site changes. Press "English" to change it back.
