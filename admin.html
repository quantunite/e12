Welcome to your panel

This is your cockpit. Here is how to fly it.

This guide opens automatically your first time and lives under the Help tab whenever you need it. Everything you change here is live on e1212.com the moment you save it.

Start here: 3 things to set up
1Paste your Traverex affiliate link. Links tab, the travelPartner row. Every travel button on the site routes there the moment you save.
2Add your videos. Trainings tab. Edit a card and paste a YouTube link (or upload a file). Mark one as Free preview so visitors get a taste.
3Check your leads daily. Leads tab. Everyone who requests an invite or signs up lands there with their name, email, and phone. A gold count on the tab means something new came in since your last look.
What each tab does
TrainingsYour academy library. Add, edit, publish, or delete video trainings. "Members only" trainings require a login to watch; "Free preview" is open to everyone. YouTube and Vimeo links play embedded; uploaded files are truly private to members. Lower Order numbers show first.
LinksEvery button on the site pulls from here: your Traverex link (travelPartner), wealth partner, membership signup, free preview video, and contact. Empty links show visitors a polite "coming soon" note, so nothing ever looks broken.
AnalyticsReal visitor numbers only: views per day, clicks on each partner link (how many people you sent to Traverex), and where visitors came from. No cookies, nothing shared with third parties.
LeadsInterest forms and signups: name, email, phone, and where each one came from. Export CSV gives you a spreadsheet. Delete removes one permanently.
MembersEveryone with an account. Promote someone to admin (they get this panel) or back to member. New registrants get a gold "new" tag since your last look.
AdminsEmails on this list become admins automatically when they create their account. For people who already have accounts, use the Members tab instead.
Good to know
The sun/moon button switches light and dark mode; visitors have the same choice, plus an EN/ES language switch on the site.
You will never get automated emails from this system. The panel badges are your notifications; make checking Leads part of your routine.
A YouTube link on a "Members" training is only softly protected, since anyone with the raw link can watch it on YouTube. Use unlisted links, or upload the file for real privacy.
Site down or something broken? That is covered under emergency maintenance: contact admin@atpconsultancy.com.
Admin emails

Anyone who creates an account with one of these emails becomes an admin automatically. To promote someone who already has an account, use the Members tab.
