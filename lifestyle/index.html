Help

Stuck on something?Start here.

How do I join?

Press any gold "Join now" button on this page. It opens the Traverex enrolment form in a new tab. Fill it in and you are a member. If you would rather just book a trip without joining, use the "Book as a customer" link instead.

What is the difference between joining and booking as a customer?

Booking as a customer means you buy travel at member prices. Joining means you also get your own link, so you earn when other people book through you. Most people join.

How do I watch a video?

Press anywhere on the picture of the lesson you want. It opens straight away. To close it, press the Close button in the corner.

I forgot my password.

Press Sign in at the top of the page, then use the reset option. If it does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder, then contact us below.

Can I do all of this in Spanish?

Yes. Press "Español" at the top of the page and the whole site changes. Press "English" to change it back.
